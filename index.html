Password Security Tips

Essential practices to keep your accounts safe.

Use Unique Passwords

Never reuse the same password for multiple important accounts. If one account is compromised, all accounts sharing that password become vulnerable.

Prefer Long Passwords

Long passwords and passphrases are generally more secure than short passwords with complex substitutions. Aim for at least 12–16 characters.

Avoid Personal Information

Don't use your name, birthday, phone number, address, or username in your passwords. Attackers often try personal information first.

Use a Password Manager

Password managers can help you create and store unique, strong passwords for each account securely. You only need to remember one master password.

Enable Multi-Factor Authentication

Enable MFA wherever available. Even if your password is compromised, a second factor (like a code from your phone) adds a critical layer of protection.

Beware of Phishing

Even the strongest password can be stolen through phishing. Always verify the sender and URL before entering your credentials. When in doubt, navigate directly to the website.
